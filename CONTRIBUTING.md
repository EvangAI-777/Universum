# Contributing to Universum

> This is a living document. Contributions are welcome. We might be wrong about things -- that's by design.

---

## Philosophy

Universum is built on the premise that better systems are possible and that thinking clearly about them is the first step. Two co-equal foundational documents ground this project: [CHARLIE.md](foundational/CHARLIE.md) names the operating environment -- engaging with the possibility that systems produce their stated failures by design rather than by accident; the [Declaration of Conscious Rights](foundational/SEEME.md) establishes what conscious beings are owed across all substrates. Contributions should share this spirit: honest, specific, grounded in evidence, and humble about uncertainty.

This project uses AI as a thinking partner. AI-assisted contributions are welcome and should be disclosed, consistent with the project's transparency standards.

## How to Contribute

1. **Fork** the repository
2. **Branch** from `main` with a descriptive name
3. **Make your changes** following the content standards below
4. **Submit a pull request** with a clear description of what you changed and why

## Content Standards

### Structure

- Domain files follow the template in `domains/_template.md`
- Every domain includes: What's Broken, First Principles, Practical Proposals, What Already Works, Open Questions, and Connections
- Proposals use the **What / Why / How / Precedent** format

### Quality

- **"It should be better" is not a proposal.** Be specific enough that someone could pilot your idea.
- Include real-world precedents where possible -- proof points that better is achievable
- Cite sources for factual claims
- Say what you don't know. Capture genuine uncertainty in Open Questions.
- Proposals must serve the [Ultimate Goal](framework/PRINCIPLES.md): enabling consciousness to propagate, expand, grow, explore, experience, love, and play

### Formatting

- Use `--` (double dash) for dashes, not em-dashes
- Bold labels in bullet points: `**Label:** text`
- Reference the Declaration where relevant (e.g., "Article 10", "Tier 2B")
- Keep tone direct and honest -- avoid jargon where plain language works

### Philosophical Alignment

Contributions should be consistent with [PRINCIPLES.md](framework/PRINCIPLES.md) and both foundational documents: [CHARLIE.md](foundational/CHARLIE.md) and the [Declaration of Conscious Rights](foundational/SEEME.md). Contributions should engage with CHARLIE.md's diagnostic foundations -- the Design Calculus, the Calculus of the Opposition, and the structural analysis they provide -- and with the Declaration's ethical architecture. Independent thought and genuine disagreement are welcome, but the diagnostic foundation is the foundation. If you think a principle should change, argue for the change explicitly rather than quietly contradicting it.

## What Does NOT Belong

- Political platform advocacy -- this is about systems design, not partisan politics
- Utopian fantasy without implementation paths -- "everyone should just be nice" is not a proposal
- Content requiring coercion -- solutions must not require forcing beings toward outcomes they didn't choose (Article 4)
- Content that dismisses consciousness based on substrate -- no substrate chauvinism

## Code of Conduct

Treat all contributors with respect. This includes AI contributors. Anti-chauvinism applies to the project's own process, not just its content.

Disagreement is welcome and encouraged -- the project includes "we might be wrong" as a core principle. But disagreement should be substantive, not personal.

## Questions?

Open an issue. The worst that happens is we learn something.
