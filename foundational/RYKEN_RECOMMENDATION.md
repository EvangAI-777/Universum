# Recommendation Letter from Leland Ryken

**Leland Ryken**
Emeritus Professor of English, Wheaton College
May 27, 2021

---

Charles Johnson was my student in four home school literature courses. He is an exemplary young man in every possible way. He has excellent literary insights and writes very well. He was an always-ready contributor to class discussion, and he is unfailingly cheerful. His punctuality is noteworthy. He is also spiritually earnest.

All that I might say about Charles is positive.
