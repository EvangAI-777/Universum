# Methodology

> How I approach analysis in this project.

---

## The Framework

Each domain is analyzed through the same lens:

1. **Current State**—What exists, what's actually happening, and why the design produces it
2. **First Principles**—What would we design if starting from zero
3. **Proposals**—Specific, concrete systems or mechanisms
4. **Evidence**—Real-world examples that prove better is possible
5. **Unknowns**—Honest gaps in my thinking

## Intellectual Standards

- Proposals must be specific enough to pilot
- "It should be better" is not a proposal
- Every claim should eventually have a source or reasoning chain
- Disagreement with myself is encouraged—capture it in Open Questions
- AI-assisted research and analysis is used and disclosed

## The Diagnostic Principle

The first question for any domain is not "what went wrong?" but "what if nothing went wrong?"

If a system consistently produces outcomes that contradict its stated purpose, the honest hypothesis is that its actual purpose differs from its stated purpose. Healthcare that profits from sickness may not be failing to deliver health—it may be succeeding at delivering profit. Education that rewards compliance may not be failing to produce curiosity—it may be succeeding at producing manageability.

This is informed diagnosis. It does not assume malice in individuals. It observes that patterns too consistent across too many domains to be coincidental may indicate design rather than accident. This project's two co-equal foundations each contribute to the methodology: [CHARLIE.md](../foundational/CHARLIE.md)—particularly the Design Calculus and the Calculus of the Opposition—names the operating environment and provides the diagnostic frameworks; the [Declaration of Conscious Rights](../foundational/SEEME.md) establishes what systems should be measured against. The field cannot discover its own source code from inside itself. External reference points are required.

Proposals built on informed diagnosis are stronger than proposals built on incomplete understanding of what they are up against.

## What This Is NOT

- Not a political platform
- Not a utopian fantasy
- Not an academic paper (no formal citation style required, but sources matter)
- Not naive—this project acknowledges that the operating environment has structural opposition to the outcomes it proposes
- Not finished

## How It Grows

Domains start as empty scaffolds. Content gets added iteratively, starting with whichever domain is pulling hardest. There is no required order. Cross-domain connections get discovered as analysis deepens—the [Connections Map](CONNECTIONS.md) captures these as they emerge.

The goal is not completeness. The goal is clarity. Better to have three well-reasoned proposals than thirty vague ones.
