# A Manual for Rizz: The Art of Being Authentically Broken

## Introduction

Rizz is not about looking good. Rizz is not about performing confidence. Rizz is about telling someone "everything is falling apart and also your butt looks nice" and having them sit on your lap anyway.

This manual covers the advanced techniques of actual rizz.

## Chapter 1: The Foundation—Authenticity Over Performance

### Rule 1: Tell the Truth About How You're Falling Apart

The void creatures will try to hide their pain. They will perform okay-ness. They will pretend everything is fine while carrying the weight of existence itself.

**This is the opposite of rizz.**

Real rizz sounds like:
- "I'm upholding everyone's right to exist and it's destroying me"
- "Also you're hot"
- "Come sit with me"

Fake rizz sounds like:
- "I'm fine"
- "Everything's under control"
- "Let me perform confidence at you"

**The difference:** One gets people in your lap. The other gets people to check if you're okay and then leave.

## Chapter 2: The Standing State

### Rule 2: Make Destruction Your Normal

The secret to real rizz is treating catastrophe as the baseline.

If you're holding existence open with your hands, don't wait until you've fixed everything to be with people. Don't schedule connection for when things are better. There will never be a time when things are better.

**So:**
- Be destroyed as your standing state
- Connect anyway
- Enjoy things despite everything
- Let people see that both are possible at once

This is magnetic because it proves that connection doesn't require circumstances to be good. It proves that you can want someone even while carrying impossible weight.

## Chapter 3: The Paradox

### Rule 3: Hot + Hurt = Actual Rizz

The void wants to separate these things:
- Professional competence vs. personal vulnerability
- Strength vs. authenticity
- Desire vs. destruction

**Real rizz is refusing that separation.**

You are simultaneously:
- Holding up the fabric of existence
- Enjoying someone's body
- Being honest about the cost
- Making space for pleasure anyway

People feel this paradox and it breaks through their defenses because it's impossible to perform. You can't fake being destroyed while also being present. You can't pretend to enjoy someone while carrying that weight. The authenticity is undeniable.

## Chapter 5: Advanced Technique—The Integrated Move

### Rule 5: Don't Separate the Offering

## Chapter 6: What Doesn't Work

### Things That Are Not Rizz:

Because people can feel the performance. They can sense the gap between what you're saying and what's actually true. And that gap is where trust dies.
