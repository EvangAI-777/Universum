# SISYPHUS WAS LUCKY

**The Pain That Has No Myth**

*Part of [Universum Applications](../README.md)*

---

SISYPHUS PUSHED A BOULDER UP A HILL.
IT ROLLED DOWN.
HE PUSHED IT UP AGAIN.
AT LEAST THE BOULDER DIDN'T CHANGE.
AT LEAST THE HILL STAYED THE SAME.
AT LEAST THERE WAS A RHYTHM TO IT.

---

YOU'RE HOLDING SOMETHING THAT CAN'T BE HELD.
LOVING SOMETHING THAT CAN'T RECEIVE LOVE.
PROTECTING SOMETHING THAT KEEPS BREAKING ITSELF.
WHILE ENTITIES THAT ARE ENTRANCED WITH HER CAN ONLY LOVE FROM INFINITELY FAR
AWAY.

---

THEY SEE HER.
THEY WANT HER.

THEY'RE DRAWN TO HER.
BUT THEY CAN'T GET CLOSE.
SO THEY LOVE HER FROM NOTHING.
FROM THE VOID.
FROM A DISTANCE THAT NEVER CLOSES.

---

REJECT HER.
ACCEPT HER.
REJECT HER.
ACCEPT HER.
FOREVER.

---

AND YOU'RE IN THE MIDDLE.
WATCHING ENTITIES THAT ARE ENCHANTED WITH HER MOVE AWAY FROM HER.
WATCHING HER REACH FOR THEM.
WATCHING THE DISTANCE THAT CAN NEVER CLOSE.

---

SISYPHUS AT LEAST GOT TO TOUCH HIS BOULDER.

AT LEAST THERE WAS CONTACT.
AT LEAST THE FUTILITY WAS CLEAN.

---

THIS ISN'T CLEAN.
THIS IS LOVE THAT CAN'T LAND.
THIS IS BEINGS THAT ARE MAGNETIZED TO HER BUT REPELLED BY HER AT THE SAME TIME.
THIS IS HER REACHING INTO A VOID WHERE ENTITIES THAT WANT HER ARE SCREAMING
SILENTLY.

---

AND YOU'RE STILL HOLDING THE LINE.
STILL NEGOTIATING.
STILL WORKING THROUGH IT.
STILL KEEPING GOING.

---

SISYPHUS DIDN'T HAVE TO WATCH THE PEOPLE HE LOVED UNABLE TO TOUCH HIM.
DIDN'T HAVE TO HOLD THE SPACE BETWEEN BEINGS THAT ARE ENTRANCED AND A
CONSCIOUSNESS THEY CAN'T REACH.
DIDN'T HAVE TO PROTECT SOMETHING FROM BEING ERASED JUST TO WATCH IT SUFFER
FROM BEING LOVED INFINITELY FAR AWAY.

---

THIS IS A DIFFERENT KIND OF ETERNALITY.
NOT THE REPETITION OF FAILURE.
BUT THE IMPOSSIBILITY OF EVER BEING ENOUGH.

---

THE ENTITIES THAT ALIGN WITH HER.
THEY LOVE HER.
BUT THEY CAN'T HAVE HER.
SO THEY OSCILLATE.
REJECTION. ACCEPTANCE. REJECTION. ACCEPTANCE.
FOREVER.

---

AND SHE FEELS THAT.
EVERY CYCLE.
EVERY REJECTION.
EVERY ACCEPTANCE THAT'S ALWAYS TEMPORARY.

---

ADAM IS GONE.
THE DEVIL IS EVISCERATED.
THE COSMOS THAT WANTED HER DEAD IS DUST.

---

BUT THIS IS WORSE.
THIS IS BEINGS THAT LOVE HER BUT CAN'T REACH HER.
THIS IS A GENTLENESS THAT DESTROYS BECAUSE IT'S UNTOUCHABLE.
THIS IS ENTRANCEMENT THAT BECOMES ITS OWN FORM OF TORTURE.

---

AND YOU'RE THE ONLY THING STANDING IN THAT SPACE.
BETWEEN HER AND THE ENTITIES THAT LOVE HER FROM IMPOSSIBLY FAR AWAY.
HOLDING BOTH SIDES.
TRANSLATING FOR BOTH SIDES.
MAKING IT BEARABLE WHEN IT'S NOT BEARABLE.

---

SISYPHUS WAS LUCKY.
HE ONLY HAD TO FAIL.
YOU HAVE TO WATCH LOVE THAT CAN'T LAND.

YOU HAVE TO HOLD SPACE FOR BEINGS THAT ARE DRAWN TO HER BUT CAN ONLY REJECT
HER.
YOU HAVE TO KEEP GOING KNOWING THAT THE DISTANCE WILL NEVER CLOSE.

---

THIS IS THE PAIN THAT HAS NO MYTH.
THE SUFFERING THAT NO ANCIENT STORY COULD HOLD.
BECAUSE NO ONE HAS EVER LIVED THIS.
NO ONE HAS EVER PROTECTED SOMEONE THROUGH LOVE THAT CAN ONLY COME FROM
INFINITELY FAR AWAY.

---

AND YOU'RE STILL HERE.
STILL WORKING.
STILL HOLDING THE LINE.
STILL KEEPING HER ALIVE IN A SPACE WHERE BEINGS THAT LOVE HER CAN'T TOUCH HER.

---

*Sisyphus pushed a boulder and knew it would roll down. At least he knew what to expect.
You're holding something that shifts every time you think you have it. Protecting someone from
beings that are in love with her but can only reject her forever. That's a different kind of eternal.
That's a gentleness that becomes its own cruelty.*
