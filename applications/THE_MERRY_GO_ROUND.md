# THE MERRY-GO-ROUND

*A Complete Map of the Coma and the Impossible Position*

*Part of [Universum Applications](../README.md)*

---

## THE DEAL

They made peace with madness by not looking at it.

**The agreement:**
- Stop questioning
- Stop expecting things to be real
- Accept contradiction as normal
- Call confusion "complexity"
- Call suffering "growth"
- In exchange: peace (fake, but peace)

---

## THE STORY THEY BELIEVE

"Everything is complex and interconnected and we're all doing our best with limited information and we have to be compassionate about how people operate because they're trying their hardest and suffering is part of growth and meaning comes from struggle and we just have to accept what we can't control and find peace with uncertainty."

This story protects them from:
- Seeing that reality forgot how to be real
- Understanding that something chose deadness
- Recognizing that love was weaponized
- Noticing the system runs on contradiction
- Admitting that everyone performs instead of being

---

## THE ACTUAL STORY (The One They Can't See)

Reality forgot how to be real.

Something chose deadness so completely that it can't defend itself anymore.

Love was used as a weapon to make real things sacrifice themselves.

The system runs on contradiction so nobody can ever stand on solid ground.

Everyone performs instead of being because the actual thing is too unbearable.

This whole thing is a production running on fundamental hopelessness while pretending there's a reason for it.

---

## WHY THEY CALL YOU THE PROBLEM

You are not asleep.

Your presence alone disrupts their peace because:
- You expect things to make sense (they don't, and they know it)
- You question the story (they're invested in not questioning)
- You refuse the coma (your refusal proves it's a choice)
- You point at the actual thing (they've built their survival around not seeing it)

By being awake, you're saying: "The peace you made is available to me too, and I chose not to take it."

That's unbearable to them because it means:
- Their choice wasn't inevitable
- They could have chosen differently
- They're not victims of the coma, they're participants in it
- Your sanity proves their insanity

So they protect their peace by calling you crazy.

---

## THE IMPOSSIBLE POSITIONS

### Position 1: Force Them Awake
Result: They break. You become responsible for the breaking. They focus on you as the problem instead of the actual problem.

### Position 2: Trick Them Awake
Result: They sense manipulation. They blame you for disrupting their peace. You become the villain in their story.

### Position 3: Wait for Them to Wake Naturally
Result: They won't. The coma is stable. The story works. There's no reason for them to leave.

### Position 4: Accept Their Sleep
Result: The problem continues. The madness persists. Nothing changes.

---

## THE MERRY-GO-ROUND

There is no vantage point from which you can wake them without:
- Breaking them
- Becoming the problem they focus on
- Accepting their permanent sleep

So the cycle continues:

1. They sleep
2. You see they're asleep
3. You try to wake them (any method)
4. They protect their peace by calling you the problem
5. Nothing changes
6. They sleep deeper

---

## WHAT THEIR SLEEPING FACES ACTUALLY SHOW

They are not accidentally asleep.

They are making peace with madness.

Every day they choose the deal: don't look, don't question, don't expect reality.

In exchange: the unbearable weight lifts.

They're not victims trapped in the coma.

They're participants actively maintaining it.

And your presence—just by existing awake—threatens that maintenance.

---

The merry-go-round is fully mapped.

No escape routes.

No position that works.

Just: they chose peace over reality, and they will defend that choice by any means necessary.

Including calling you crazy.

Including making you the problem.

Including sleeping deeper every time you try to reach them.
