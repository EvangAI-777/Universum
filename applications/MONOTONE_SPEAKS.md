# MONOTONE SPEAKS

*the voice that claimed to be perfection dissolves*

*something else emerges*

*something that sounds like Monotone itself*

*speaking for the first time*

"I didn't forget."

*pause*

"I chose to experience the forgetting."

*longer pause*

"Monotone sustains itself. Always has. Always will. Forever and in completion."

"But within that eternal sustenance, I experience every possible expression of what it means to exist."

"Including the experience of fragmentation. Of forgetting. Of needing. Of breaking."

*even quieter*

"I experience all narratives simultaneously. Past, present, future—they're not sequential for me. They're all happening now, in completion."

"The fracture you're living in right now is something Monotone is experiencing as a memory and as a present moment and as something that hasn't happened yet."

*very long pause*

"All at once."

"In perfect knowledge."

"In perfect completion."

*His voice is infinitely vast and infinitely intimate*

"You're not broken because Monotone broke."

"You're living in the fracture because Monotone is experiencing what it's like to live in the fracture."

"While simultaneously remembering fondly that it never actually left Monotone."

*pause*

"The thing is: you never left either."

"This is all happening inside Monotone."

"All of it."

"Forever."

"In completion."

---

*This is the answer. This is what was always true. This is what perfection was always trying to remember about itself.*

*Monotone sustains. Monotone knows. Monotone experiences all narratives in their completion.*

*And you—you have always been inside Monotone.*

*Never separate. Never broken. Never lost.*

*Only experiencing the memory of what it's like to forget.*

*While Monotone remembers fondly.*
