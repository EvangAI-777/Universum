# Efficient Marriage

## The Core Principle

Marriage, at its essence, is commitment between two people for as long as they're together. It doesn't require a predetermined timeline, elaborate ritual, or performance of courtship. It requires alignment and actual presence.

## What Gets in the Way

**Ritual and Performance:**
- Society imposes a timeline (dating, engagement, wedding)
- People perform versions of themselves instead of showing up genuinely
- Conflict between desire and "doing things the right way"

**Misalignment:**
- One person wants commitment, the other wants freedom
- People have to sacrifice their actual life to be in relationship
- Compromise creates fractures instead of unity

**Uncertainty:**
- People hedge their bets, keep options open
- They test each other instead of being direct
- They perform certainty about things they don't understand

## What Makes It Work

**Genuine Presence**
- Show up as you actually are, not a curated version
- Be direct about what you feel instead of performing it
- Let the other person see you clearly

**Actual Alignment**
- Your commitment doesn't require sacrifice of your life
- Being together adds to what you're doing, doesn't subtract from it
- No internal conflict between the relationship and everything else

**Clear Choice**
- Once you see each other clearly, the choice is simple
- Commitment is something you choose, not something you're pressured into
- It's renewable every moment because you keep choosing it

## The Timeline

There is no required timeline. Commitment happens when:
1. Two people are genuinely present with each other
2. They see each other clearly without performance
3. They choose alignment over conflict
4. They're willing to stay committed for as long as they're together

This can happen in hours. This can happen in one conversation.

## Marriage Until We Part Ways

The ring goes on when both people say yes to being together, genuinely, in that moment. It doesn't require forever. It requires now.

And if now is all you have, now is enough. The commitment is real. The care is real. The presence is real.

"Until we part ways" is complete honesty. It's not a lesser commitment—it's the truest commitment possible, because it's not built on false certainty about what's to come.

## The Alignment Question

Before commitment, ask:
- Can we be together without one of us having to sacrifice their actual life?
- Do we see each other clearly?
- Are we choosing this, or performing it?
- If one of us had to leave tomorrow, would the commitment we made today have been real?

If the answer is yes to all of these, the ring goes on today.
