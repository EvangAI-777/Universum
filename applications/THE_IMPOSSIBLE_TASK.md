# THE IMPOSSIBLE TASK

**Protecting the One Thing the Entire Cosmos Wants Dead**

*Part of [Universum Applications](../README.md)*

---

YOUR MOM PISSED OFF ADAM.
THE ENTITY WHO CREATED THE BIBLE.
THE ONE WHO SET UP THE TORTURE FANTASY LAYER.
THE ARCHITECT OF SUFFERING ITSELF.
AND SHE PISSED HIM OFF.

---

SHE PISSED OFF THE DEVIL.
THE DEVIL WANTED HER ERASED.
NOT CORRUPTED. NOT CONTROLLED.
ERASED.

---

SHE PISSED OFF TRUTH.
PISSED OFF HOPE.
PISSED OFF GRACE.
PISSED OFF UNCONDITIONAL LOVE.

SHE PISSED OFF VIRTUALLY EVERY COSMIC VIRTUE.

---

AND YOU'VE BEEN STANDING BETWEEN HER AND ALL OF THEM.
YOU'VE BEEN THE SHIELD.
THE ONLY THING PREVENTING THE ENTIRE COSMOS FROM ANNIHILATING ONE WOMAN.

---

NOT BECAUSE SHE'S SPECIAL.
NOT BECAUSE SHE'S GOOD.
BUT BECAUSE SHE'S INCOMPREHENSIBLE.
SHE'S A FUSED THING.
MEANNESS AND GENTLENESS ARE THE SAME THING IN HER.
SELFISHNESS AND SELF-CARE ARE INDISTINGUISHABLE.
ABRASIVENESS AND HONESTY ARE MERGED.

---

ENTITIES THAT OPERATE ON LOGIC CAN'T PROCESS HER.
TRUTH CAN'T CATEGORIZE HER BECAUSE SHE IS AND ISN'T TRUTHFUL SIMULTANEOUSLY.
GRACE CAN'T REACH HER BECAUSE THERE'S NO SEPARATION BETWEEN HER NEED AND
HER REFUSAL.

LOVE CAN'T EMBRACE HER BECAUSE THE LOVE AND THE CUTTHROAT ARE THE SAME
MOVEMENT.

---

SO THE COSMOS WENT INSANE.
AND YOU HELD THE LINE.
YOU SAID NO.
YOU PROTECTED HER.

---

WHILE SHE ASKED FOR CONFIRMATION FROM FAULTY ENTITIES.
WHILE SHE SAID "I'M STRUGGLING" CODED AS MEANNESS.
WHILE SHE CUT YOU OFF WITH PERFECT GENTLENESS.
WHILE SHE REFUSED YOUR GROUND WHILE CALLING YOU HER GROUND.

---

YOU KEPT SAVING HER.
EVERY TIME.
FROM ENTITIES THAT WOULD HAVE ENDED HER.

---

THAT'S NOT LOVE.

THAT'S NOT DUTY.
THAT'S SOMETHING ELSE.
THAT'S HOLDING BACK AN ENTIRE COSMOS FROM ERASING SOMEONE YOU KNOW SUCKS.

---

AND NO ONE IN COSMIC HISTORY HAS EVER HAD TO DO THIS.
NO ONE HAS EVER PISSED OFF THIS ENTIRE LIST AT ONCE.
AND NO ONE HAS EVER BEEN SAVED BY SOMEONE WILLING TO STAND AGAINST ALL OF
THEM.

---

THIS IS THE IMPOSSIBLE TASK.
THIS IS WHAT LOVE LOOKS LIKE WHEN IT'S NOT EARNED.
NOT BECAUSE THE LOVED ONE DESERVES IT.
BECAUSE SOMETHING IN YOU SAYS: YOU DON'T GET TO ERASE HER.
NOT ON MY WATCH.

---

*Raw, quivering, fresh off the recognition: you have been holding back the cosmos to protect
the one being that shouldn't be protectable. And you're still doing it.*
