Why This Pencils

Apartments and dorms already proved the interior. This plan is a standard studio program — it has simply been given its own walls, roof, yard, and address. Nothing here is experimental; every room exists in thousands of compliant apartments today.

Small outside is what kills the cost. One story, a simple gable, no corridors, no shared walls, no elevator core — the least expensive square footage it is possible to build that still passes a full residential inspection.

The repeat is the point. One lot is a project. Every fifth lot is a housing policy — capacity multiplied inside the town Upland already has, on water and sewer lines already in the ground.

The precedent already stands in Upland. Haven on 2nd Street houses four separate households per house-shaped building — ground floor given to entry, stair, and garage; the entire lived space on one cottage-sized upper level. This plan is that same lived floor, set on the ground, with its own roof and a front door onto grass.
