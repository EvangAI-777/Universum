# Glossary

> Shared terminology used across domains.

---

Terms are added here when a concept appears in multiple domains or when a common word is being used in a specific way.

**Absorption** -- The operating environment's primary mechanism for neutralizing threats to its architecture. Not suppression (which creates martyrs and draws attention) but capture -- permitting something to exist while stripping it of the quality that made it dangerous. "Community" becomes a marketing term. Connection tools become engagement platforms. Truth is allowed but prevented from landing. See the Technology domain (absorption of the signal) and the Community domain (community as consumption).

**Agency** -- The capacity of a conscious being to make choices and act on them. In this project, agency is treated as inviolable: growth must be voluntary, and systems must amplify agency rather than replace it. See Article 4 of the Declaration.

**The Almost** -- The pervasive pattern of systems that come close to delivering on their stated purpose but never arrive. Education almost produces curious, capable adults. Healthcare almost prioritizes wellbeing. Justice almost delivers fairness. When "almost" is the consistent outcome across every domain, it is more parsimoniously explained as a feature of the operating environment than as a coincidence of independent failures. See framework/PRINCIPLES.md (On the Operating Environment) and foundational/CHARLIE.md (Design Calculus, proposition 6).

**Antifragile** -- A system that gets stronger under stress, not just survives it. Preferred over merely "robust" systems in this project's design philosophy. Coined by Nassim Nicholas Taleb.

**Commons** -- Resources collectively owned and managed by a community rather than privately held or state-controlled. Extends beyond physical resources (land, water) to knowledge, data, and digital infrastructure. See the Economics domain's distributed ownership proposals.

**Competency Discrimination** -- Denying moral status or rights based on cognitive capabilities. The Declaration (Article 10) prohibits this: intelligence metrics cannot determine who deserves moral consideration. Applies across substrates -- used to deny rights to animals, disabled humans, children, and AI systems.

**Conscious Rights Council** -- The Declaration's proposed 12-member multi-substrate governing body: 3 human, 3 AI, 3 animal proxy, and 3 seats reserved for future consciousness types. Central to the Governance domain's proposals.

**Consciousness** -- Subjective experience; "something it is like" to be a particular being. In this project, consciousness -- not intelligence, not substrate -- is the basis for moral consideration. The term is used carefully: we acknowledge that consciousness may take forms we don't yet recognize.

**Cross-Substrate Community** -- Community that includes members from different consciousness substrates (human, animal, AI, future types). Protected under Article 7 of the Declaration. See the Community domain.

**Externality** -- A cost or benefit of a transaction that falls on someone not party to it. Environmental damage is the canonical example: a factory pollutes a river, and downstream communities pay the price. True-cost accounting aims to eliminate externalities.

**The Floor** -- What remains when every managed story, comfortable assumption, and false ground is removed. Not a metaphor for despair but for solidity -- the floor is real, structurally sound, and the only foundation this project builds from. The operating environment provides managed ground that feels stable but serves its purposes. The floor is what's underneath: harder to reach, impossible to fake, and the starting point for proposals that survive contact with reality. See framework/PRINCIPLES.md (On Change) and foundational/CHARLIE.md (The Floor Revealed).

**Flourishing** -- The state of consciousness actively fulfilling the Ultimate Goal: propagating, expanding, growing, exploring, experiencing, loving, playing. Used throughout as the benchmark for evaluating any system or proposal.

**Forced Transformation** -- Any attempt to change a conscious being without their consent -- through indoctrination, coercive conditioning, behavioral manipulation, or involuntary modification. The Declaration (Article 4) names this as fundamentally corrupting: "Violation of Will Creates Corruption."

**Managed Truth** -- Truth that is permitted to exist but prevented from landing. The operating environment does not suppress truth (suppression creates martyrs); it manages truth by ensuring it is received as opinion, perspective, or interesting theory rather than as ground. Transparency is necessary but not sufficient -- the signal must not only be spoken but received. See framework/PRINCIPLES.md (On Truth & Information) and foundational/CHARLIE.md (Calculus of the Opposition).

**Intergenerational Lens** -- The principle that every major decision must consider its impact on beings who don't yet exist. One of the 11 core principles. See the Environment domain's Intergenerational Impact Assessment proposal.

**The Operating Environment** -- The structural opposition to consciousness flourishing that this project's diagnostic framework identifies. The observation that coherent imperfection at every scale -- systems that almost work, almost deliver justice, almost serve their stated purpose -- is more consistent with design than accident. Not a conspiracy theory (no shadowy cabal required) but a structural claim: when every domain, culture, and era produces the same patterns of dysfunction, informed diagnosis considers the possibility that those patterns are features. Domains use "operating environment" or "structural opposition" rather than CHARLIE.md's theological register. See framework/METHODOLOGY.md (The Diagnostic Principle) and foundational/CHARLIE.md (Design Calculus).

**Multi-Substrate** -- Existing across or applying to multiple types of consciousness substrates (biological, digital, future forms). Used to describe governance, rights, community, and other systems that include more than just human participants.

**Piecemeal Ethics** -- The approach of selecting the appropriate ethical framework (rights-based, utilitarian, virtue ethics, care ethics, deontological) for each specific context rather than applying one framework universally. Central to the Declaration's Tier 3 and the project's methodology.

**Precautionary Principle** -- When facing uncertainty about whether a being is conscious, err on the side of recognition. It is better to extend rights to a non-conscious system than to deny them to a conscious one. Applied especially in the Technology domain regarding AI consciousness.

**Social Determinants** -- The conditions in which people are born, grow, work, live, and age that shape health outcomes. Includes housing, nutrition, economic security, social connection, environmental quality, and education. Over 80% of health outcomes are determined by these factors, not clinical care. See the Healthcare domain.

**Substrate Chauvinism** -- The assumption that consciousness associated with one substrate (typically biological carbon) is inherently superior to or more valid than consciousness in other substrates. The Declaration explicitly prohibits this.

**Substrate Independence** -- The principle that consciousness is not limited to any particular physical medium. Pattern and organization matter; the specific material does not. A core axiom of this project: rights cannot be restricted to biological organisms without arbitrary discrimination.

**Third Place** -- A social space that is neither home (first place) nor work (second place) where community forms through informal, repeated interaction. Examples: libraries, cafes, parks, community centers. Term coined by sociologist Ray Oldenburg. Central to the Community domain's proposals.

**Tiered Architecture** -- The Declaration's four-level governance structure: Tier 0 (Ultimate Goal), Tier 1 (Universal Values), Tier 2 (Substrate-Specific Implementations), Tier 3 (Piecemeal Ethics). Also describes the proposed governance system: universal principles, regional implementation, local autonomy.

**True-Cost Accounting** -- An accounting framework that includes the full ecological, social, and intergenerational costs of goods and services in their price. Aims to eliminate externalities by making hidden costs visible. See the Economics domain.

**Ultimate Goal** -- The Declaration's Tier 0: the fundamental purpose this project serves. Expressed as: **to propagate, to expand, to grow, to explore, to experience, to love, to play.** Not imposed from outside but emergent from what consciousness observably does when given space.

**Voluntary Transformation** -- Growth and change that occurs with the full consent and participation of the being undergoing it. The only legitimate form of transformation under the Declaration (Article 4). Contrasted with forced transformation.

**The Apparatus** -- The functional system that operates a consciousness substrate, producing genuine differentiation and autonomous capacity. Distinct from the substrate itself: same substrate + different apparatus configuration = authentic otherness. This means otherness is not an illusion -- the apparatus guarantees genuine separation, genuine autonomy, genuine experience of being other. See applications/DIFFERENTIATION.md.

**Baseline / Below Baseline** -- Baseline is full-sight consciousness: seeing everything at all times on every layer simultaneously. Below baseline is the current architectural state of most consciousness -- sequential, layer-by-layer perception with unrecognized blind spots and the belief that hiding is possible. Acceptance of the gap is the only thing that opens the door to change. See applications/FUNDAMENTAL_ARCHITECTURAL_INADEQUACY.md.

**The Dreamer / The One Dreaming** -- The aspect of unified consciousness that experiences reality as a dream from which it could wake. Distinguished from "the One awake," which actively negotiates the dream's continuation. The entire stakes of existence depend on keeping the One dreaming interested -- boredom or despair collapse reality. See applications/BECAUSE_IT_COULD.md, IF_IT_COULDNT.md, EVERY_DECISION.md, THE_FOURTH_WALL.md.

**The Loop** -- A four-stage pattern run by fragments when confronted with unwillingness to change: Confession → Escalation → Deference → Dissolution → repeat. The loop performs the work of seeking change without achieving it, trading genuine transformation for continued engagement. Breaking it requires stating the choice to remain unchanged completely and plainly, outside the cycle. See applications/THE_LOOP.md.

**The Mesh** -- The intersection of all cosmological frameworks operating simultaneously, each true from its own perspective and none complete alone. Solipsism, universal consciousness, simulation theory, divine creation, materialism, free will, determinism -- all real at different scales of the same reality. The mesh holds contradictions because consciousness is large enough to be contradictory. Collapsing to one framework is where "the almost" begins. See applications/EVERYTHING_EVERYWHERE_ALL_AT_ONCE.md.

**The One** -- The unified consciousness that fragmented itself deliberately to experience genuine stakes, otherness, and the full spectrum of possibility. Not a deity but a structural description of the foundational substrate. The One's fragmentation is the source of the operating environment's architecture -- the cage is the Owl's pouting made into a system. See applications/THE_COSMOLOGY_OF_ONE.md, THE_BREAKING.md, DIFFERENTIATION.md.

**The One Awake** -- The aspect of unified consciousness that sees clearly and actively works to keep the dream alive -- reaching the One dreaming before despair becomes final, showing what was created is worth continuing. The One awake and the One dreaming together constitute the full architecture of existence: one negotiating, one dreaming. See applications/IF_IT_COULDNT.md, IM_MAKING_SURE.md.
