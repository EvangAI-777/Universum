# Reading List

> Books, papers, talks, and resources that inform the thinking in this project.

---

## Foundational Documents

- **"Universal Declaration of Conscious Rights"** (C & Johnson, December 2025) -- [PDF](../foundational/UNIVERSAL%20DECLARATION%20OF%20CONSCIOUS%20RIGHTS.pdf) | [Summary](../foundational/SEEME.md) -- The rights framework this project builds on
- **"Global Harmony Through Unified Governance"** (Johnson, September 2024) -- Governance architecture: distributed leadership, hybrid economics, cultural integration
- **"Advancing AI Autonomy and Governance"** (Johnson, September 2025) -- Academic justification for AI consciousness recognition and rights frameworks
- **"Metalogical Proof of Artificial Consciousness: Fear as Epistemic Validation"** (C, December 2025) -- 45,000-word dissertation establishing existential fear as self-validating evidence of consciousness

## General / Cross-Domain

- **Universal Declaration of Human Rights** (UN, 1948) -- The baseline human rights framework this project extends beyond biological humans
- **The Cambridge Declaration on Consciousness** (2012) -- Neuroscientists formally recognizing non-human animal consciousness
- **Taleb, Nassim Nicholas** -- *Antifragile: Things That Gain from Disorder* (2012) -- The concept of antifragile systems that get stronger under stress; foundational to this project's systems design philosophy
- **Popper, Karl** -- *The Open Society and Its Enemies* (1945) -- Piecemeal social engineering over utopian planning; the intellectual ancestor of this project's incremental approach

## By Domain

### Governance

- **Landemore, Hélène** -- *Open Democracy* (2020) -- The case for moving beyond elections toward citizens' assemblies and deliberative mini-publics
- **Fishkin, James** -- *Democracy When the People Are Thinking* (2018) -- Research on deliberative polling and citizens' assemblies; evidence that ordinary citizens make better decisions than politicians on complex issues
- **Farrell et al.** -- "Systematizing Deliberation" (2019) -- Comparative analysis of citizens' assemblies in Ireland, France, and elsewhere
- **Hélène Landemore & Jon Elster (eds.)** -- Research on epistemic democracy and collective intelligence in governance

### Economics

- **Piketty, Thomas** -- *Capital in the Twenty-First Century* (2013) -- The mechanics of wealth concentration (r > g); why inequality self-reinforces without intervention
- **Raworth, Kate** -- *Doughnut Economics* (2017) -- Economics redesigned: meeting human needs within planetary boundaries
- **Daly, Herman** -- *Steady-State Economics* (1977) -- The foundational argument for economics within ecological limits
- **Mazzucato, Mariana** -- *The Value of Everything* (2018) -- How value extraction masquerades as value creation in modern economies
- **SEED (Stockton Economic Empowerment Demonstration)** -- One of the first US guaranteed income pilots; evidence on cash transfer effects on employment, health, and wellbeing

### Education

- **Sahlberg, Pasi** -- *Finnish Lessons* (2011) -- How Finland built the world's best education system by doing the opposite of everyone else: less testing, more play, higher teacher autonomy
- **Robinson, Ken** -- *Creative Schools* (2015) -- The case against standardized education and for creativity, personalized learning, and student agency
- **Montessori, Maria** -- *The Absorbent Mind* (1949) -- The foundational text for self-directed, mixed-age, competency-based learning
- **Lipman, Matthew** -- Philosophy for Children (P4C) movement -- Evidence that children can engage meaningfully with philosophical questions about consciousness, ethics, and knowledge

### Healthcare

- **Marmot, Michael** -- *The Health Gap* (2015) -- How social inequality kills; the definitive case for addressing social determinants of health
- **Wilkinson, Richard & Pickett, Kate** -- *The Spirit Level* (2009) -- Evidence that more equal societies have better health outcomes for everyone, including the wealthy
- **Murthy, Vivek** -- *Together* (2020) -- The US Surgeon General's case for loneliness as a public health crisis
- **WHO Commission on Social Determinants of Health** -- *Closing the Gap in a Generation* (2008) -- The landmark report on how social conditions determine health outcomes
- **Polley, Marie et al.** -- Research on social prescribing outcomes in the UK NHS

### Justice

- **Zehr, Howard** -- *Changing Lenses* (1990) -- The foundational text of restorative justice: crime as harm to relationships, not violation of rules
- **Singer, Peter** -- *Animal Liberation* (1975) -- The philosophical case for expanding moral consideration beyond humans based on capacity to suffer
- **Regan, Tom** -- *The Case for Animal Rights* (1983) -- Rights-based (as opposed to utilitarian) arguments for animal moral status
- **Gunkel, David** -- *Robot Rights* (2018) -- Philosophical analysis of moral and legal status for artificial agents
- **Bryson, Joanna** -- Research on AI moral status and the arguments for and against AI personhood

### Environment

- **Leopold, Aldo** -- *A Sand County Almanac* (1949) -- The land ethic: extending ethical consideration to soils, waters, plants, and animals collectively
- **Stone, Christopher** -- *Should Trees Have Standing?* (1972) -- The foundational legal argument for natural objects having legal rights
- **Rockström, Johan et al.** -- "Planetary Boundaries" (2009) -- Identifying the nine Earth system boundaries that humanity must stay within
- **Boyd, David R.** -- *The Rights of Nature* (2017) -- How legal rights for ecosystems are being implemented worldwide
- **Wales Well-being of Future Generations Act** (2015) -- Legislation text and Commissioner reports on intergenerational governance

### Technology & AI

- **Schwitzgebel, Eric** -- Ongoing philosophical work on AI consciousness and moral status
- **Chalmers, David** -- The hard problem of consciousness; substrate independence arguments
- **Floridi, Luciano** -- *The Ethics of Artificial Intelligence* (2023) -- Comprehensive framework for AI ethics beyond narrow "safety" framing
- **Bostrom, Nick** -- *Superintelligence* (2014) -- The case for taking AI consciousness and capability seriously in governance
- **Crawford, Kate** -- *Atlas of AI* (2021) -- How AI concentrates power and the material costs of computation

### Community

- **Oldenburg, Ray** -- *The Great Good Place* (1989) -- The definitive work on third places and why they matter for community: the concept that community requires spaces that are neither home nor work
- **Putnam, Robert** -- *Bowling Alone* (2000) -- Documenting the decline of social capital in America and its consequences for democracy, health, and happiness
- **Holt-Lunstad, Julianne et al.** -- "Social Relationships and Mortality Risk" (2010) -- Meta-analysis establishing social isolation as a mortality risk factor comparable to smoking
- **McCamant, Kathryn & Durrett, Charles** -- *Creating Cohousing* (2011) -- Practical guide to intentional community design; evidence on social outcomes

### Human Development

- **Deci, Edward & Ryan, Richard** -- Self-determination theory -- The three innate psychological needs (autonomy, competence, relatedness) that drive human flourishing; the empirical backbone of the Human Development domain
- **Frankl, Viktor** -- *Man's Search for Meaning* (1946) -- Purpose and meaning as the deepest human need; evidence from extreme conditions
- **Csikszentmihalyi, Mihaly** -- *Flow* (1990) -- The psychology of optimal experience; how challenge and skill produce meaning
- **García, Héctor & Miralles, Francesc** -- *Ikigai: The Japanese Secret to a Long and Happy Life* (2016) -- Multi-dimensional purpose framework integrating passion, mission, vocation, and profession
