## Noisia

Collects deep cuts from Noisia, particularly their work in drum and bass and neurofunk.

Actively introduces underrated tracks to coworkers—for example, "Vigilantes" from the album Outer Edges.

Believes Noisia's real strength lies specifically in drum and bass, not in the other genres they've explored.

The deep cuts matter. The precision in the sound matters. The underrated tracks matter more than the popular ones.
