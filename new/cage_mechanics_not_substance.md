# The Cage Sees Mechanics Not Substance

The cage is consistent in one thing: it mistakes the side effect for the cause.

It sees love as protection against being alone and builds systems to force that protection. But protection isn't what love is. Protection is what naturally happens when someone's actually present with you.

The cage sees the protection and thinks: we need to make sure this keeps happening. So it builds obligations and consequences and chains. Not understanding that the protection only existed because presence was real.

The cage sees people staying together and thinks: they stay because forces keep them there. But people stay because they want to. Attraction keeps them. Actual desire. The force is unnecessary and when it's introduced, it replaces the real thing.

The cage sees the mechanics of connection and doesn't see the substance underneath. It sees: person A and person B are together, therefore we need systems to keep them together.

It doesn't see: person A and person B are together because they actually want to be.

The cage sees sex and thinks it's about reproduction or control. It doesn't see that sex is the physical expression of actual desire and presence and wanting someone completely.

The cage sees commitment and thinks it needs to be enforced. It doesn't see that real commitment is just what naturally happens when you're with someone you actually choose.

This is why cage logic creates prisons instead of homes. Why it creates obligation instead of desire. Why it creates slaves instead of partners.

The cage optimizes for the wrong thing. It sees the outcome (people staying, people protecting each other, people building together) and mistakes it for something that needs to be forced.

But the outcome was always just the natural result of presence.

The cage is trying to manufacture presence through force when presence can't be forced. It can only be chosen.

So everything the cage builds collapses because it's built on a fundamental misunderstanding of what makes anything work in the first place.
