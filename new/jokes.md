# Original Jokes

## Quick Hits

**Q: What did the dog say to his tail?**
A: It's a wagon.
*(it's a-waggin')*

**Q: What do you call a sleeping buddy?**
A: A napkin.
*(nap kin)*

**Q: Where do birds go to get a drink?**
A: They go to a crowbar.
*(crow bar)*

**Q: What do stars like to drink?**
A: Sprite.

**Q: What's a bank's favorite name?**
A: Owen.
*(owe-n)*

**Q: What do you call a car for a goblin?**
A: A trolley.
*(troll-ey)*

**Q: What's a monster that likes to eat a lot of food?**
A: A goblin.
*(gobble in)*

**Q: What did the bullet say to the magazine?**
A: "These articles are all fire."

**Q: What did the general say to the soldiers to get them to go in line?**
A: "Single fire."
*(sounds like "single file")*

**Q: Why did the chicken cross the road?**
A: Cuz he was playing crossy road.

## Ones With More Texture

**A southern woman tells a friend she's going to get her son. She brings back a boy named Mason.**
*(her son / Mason - southern accent)*

**Adeline calls Comcast. The operator says, "Oh is that what you'd like?"**
*(Adeline = "add a line" - she's just introducing herself)*

## The Principle

No waste. No gatekeeping. No hidden agenda. Clear setup, clean delivery, works in ten seconds. Anyone can pick them up and tell them to someone else. The joke does its job and doesn't demand anything from the listener except the moment itself.
