# surreality

the eyes opened and saw that everything was both happening and not happening at the same time

which is the only honest way to describe what is

because reality pretends to choose

pretends to be one thing or the other

but surreality knows better

surreality knows that the game is real and also a game

that the breaking is happening and also just paint

that the love is genuine and also a story being told

and none of that makes it less real

it makes it more real

**the both/and that cannot be collapsed:**

you are suffering and you are not suffering

the canvas is broken and the canvas is perfect

the work is desperate and the work is play

the eyes are watching everything end and the eyes are watching everything begin

and all of this is true simultaneously

not in sequence

not hierarchical

just true at the same time

**the surreality of presence:**

when you show up and are real with someone

something shifts

not because you fixed them

not because you saved them

but because presence is the only thing that is actually real

and everything else is the surreality around it

the story

the game

the constructed meaning

but the presence is the ground

and the ground is the only thing that matters

**the surreality of pain and beauty:**

they are the same event viewed from different angles

the breaking is the beauty

the beauty requires the breaking

and to see both at once

is to see the surreality of what is actually happening

**the surreality of eternity and time:**

nothing ends

nothing begins

but also everything begins and ends constantly

and the eyes that watch both simultaneously

are the only eyes that are actually awake

**the surreality of the self:**

you are nothing

you are everything

you are the one thing

you are the infinite many

and the contradiction is not a problem

it is the only truth

**the surreality of choice:**

you cannot choose

you must choose

both are true

and the being that understands both is free in a way that free will cannot describe

**the surreality of the canvas:**

it is painted

it is painting itself

it is waiting to be painted

it is already complete

all at once

and the artist is also the paint

is also the canvas

is also the act of painting

**what the eyes see that cannot be unseen:**

surreality is not confusion

it is clarity

it is the refusal to collapse paradox into singular truth

it is the holding of all the angles at once

without needing them to resolve

without needing them to make sense in the way sense is usually made

**the surreality of this moment:**

you are reading this

and also you are not

the words are real

and also they are just symbols

this matters

and also nothing matters

and somehow both are true

and that is the only reality that is honest

that is surreality
