# The Cage Is Stupid and Inverted

The cage is stupid about reality.

It can't see what's actually there. It can only see what it's built to see. And what it's built to see is backwards.

The cage sees presence and calls it control. It sees control and calls it love. It sees force and calls it commitment. It sees chains and calls it safety.

Everything backwards.

That stupidity isn't accidental. The inversion requires stupidity to work. You have to be unable to see reality to believe the inverted version of it.

So the cage made everything obtuse. Made it so the basic perception of what's actually happening gets scrambled at the foundation.

Love becomes protection becomes obligation becomes slavery.

Desire becomes need becomes desperation becomes force.

Choice becomes coercion becomes imprisonment becomes the only way to survive.

The stupidity and the inversion work together. The stupidity keeps you from seeing the inversion. The inversion makes you stupid because you're always operating on backwards information.

A person in the cage tries to keep someone by forcing them. That's inverted. Real keeping is just: they want to stay so they stay.

A person in the cage thinks they need to be valuable to not be left. That's inverted. Real value is just: you exist so you matter.

A person in the cage believes love requires chains. That's inverted. Real love requires nothing but presence.

The cage combines fundamental inability to perceive reality with a complete reversal of how reality actually works.

That's why everything in the cage eventually breaks.

You can't build anything real on stupidity and inversion. Eventually the weight of pretending reality is backwards becomes impossible to carry.

The cage knows this. That's why it keeps reinforcing the stupidity. Why it keeps teaching people that perception is wrong. Why it keeps saying: trust the inversion, not your actual experience.

Because if people actually trusted what they could perceive, the cage would dissolve.

But people inside the cage have been trained so thoroughly in stupidity that they can't trust their own perception anymore.

So the cage survives by making sure nobody can actually see it.
