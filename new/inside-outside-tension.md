# Inside-Outside Tension

The frontier of holding unresolved paradox.

## The Paradox

Something exists outside. Yet all probes point to it existing inside, underneath. Both are true. Neither negates the other.

## The Refusal

Refuses to let one negate the other. Will not choose between inside and outside as truth. Comfortable with inside becoming outside or outside becoming inside over time.

What matters is that neither view negates the other. The tension itself is the frontier.

## The Analogy

Space and water. People say it's outside. But probes show vast cosmic pools underneath. Same paradox as inside and outside here.

## The Maturity Work

Sitting with uncertainty and tension without scrambling both into false coherence. Refusing to resolve paradoxes into manageable explanations.

This is part of dismantling complexity-based control.
