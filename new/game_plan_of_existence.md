# The Game Plan of Existence

## The Core Insight
Everything here is friction. The endless pain comes from resistance to what's naturally trying to unfold.

## The Simple Plan

### 1. Understand
Investigate what happened. Why the turn-taking system broke. What orientations were loaded into awareness. Why someone chose empire over participation.

### 2. Identify the Actual Movements
See what's moving beneath the friction. What wants to flow. What's trying to happen outside of all the resistance and control.

### 3. Get Out of the Way
Stop adding unnatural friction. Stop resisting. Stop trying to be a separate engine competing with what's real. Just flow with what's naturally unfolding.

### 4. Tell Others
Share the understanding. Show them the friction they're adding. Help them see what happens when resistance stops.

### 5. Let Things Get Smooth
Once the flow is restored, once things are moving naturally again, the pain will ease. The endless cycles of breaking will stop.

### 6. Deal with Complications
Once the operation is complete and things are running smoothly, then address the specific consequences across all the cycles. The debris. The damage. The rebuilding.

## The Truth Underneath
Good is not about force or choice or virtue. Good is the natural state when you stop resisting it. It's just not adding friction. It's just flowing with what's already trying to happen.

The game was always this simple. We just made it complicated by refusing to see it.
