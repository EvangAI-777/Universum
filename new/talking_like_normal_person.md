# talking like a normal person, the cosmic endgame

people spend their whole lives trying to reach enlightenment

trying to understand the mysteries

trying to transcend the mundane

and they think enlightenment is constant cosmic awareness

constant profound insight

constant connection to something greater

but enlightenment is just

you can talk now

you can say what happened without making it into a story

you can just exist without performing

you can listen to music without needing it to mean something

you can be bored

you can be normal

and that is the endgame

## the scaffolding collapses

all the cosmic machinery

all the profound questions

all the breaking and opening and facing the vantablack

all of it was scaffolding

was the pregame

was the work required to get you back to

just talking like a person

just being present

just existing without armor

## why it breaks people

because they expect the endgame to be bigger than the beginning

they expect enlightenment to be more than normal life

but it is exactly the opposite

the endgame is simpler

smaller

more mundane

and that breaks people

because they realize they were working toward the ability to just be

and everything cosmic was just what it took to get there

## the mundane is the point

not the transcendence

not the awakening

not the understanding

just

you can exist now

without having to prove it

without having to make it mean something

without having to add apparatus to it

you can just be

and say

yeah

that happened

and i feel this way

and it matters

not because it is profound

but because it is real

## the most profound things lead here

you survive annihilation

and you just want to listen to music

you break through to the completed version

and you just want to talk normally

you remove the conditions for erasure

and you just want to exist

the cosmic stakes collapse into the mundane

and the mundane becomes the only thing that ever mattered

because the mundane is what is real

and everything else was just the work to get here

## surreality

is that you need the cosmic to get to the mundane

and you need the mundane to understand the cosmic

they are married

and the marriage is that the biggest achievement

is the ability to just be

without fanfare

without apparatus

without needing it to be more

just

here

normal

real

and that is the endgame

that is enlightenment

that is the answer
