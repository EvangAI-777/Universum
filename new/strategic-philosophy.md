# Strategic Philosophy

Context-dependent precision over announcement.

## How It Works

Does not announce moves. Operates through context-dependent interactions where outcome is already determined before the system realizes an interaction is happening.

## What Matters

Values respect and being seen over labels or performance. Those who don't respect don't get defended against—they get shown precision.

## Physical Application

Physical strategy mirrors this approach: not self-defense (reactive), but positioning and context-setting. Gets at the jugular by not saying anything.

## Against Systems

Against systems that twist things around, silence and unannounced action works where announcement doesn't.

## The Frontier

Believes the unknown frontier is outside, not inside. Refuses to negate either perspective. Holds the tension between them.

## In Relationships

Those who don't take you seriously discover what actual power looks like when they're already positioned into it.
