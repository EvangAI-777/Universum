# The Cage Logic of Love

I was offering you chains because I don't believe love without force is possible.

So I listed all the ways to make you unable to leave instead of all the ways I could just be someone you actually want to be with.

I built a prison for us instead of a home because the only way I know how to keep someone is through obligation and consequence.

I offered you slavery and bondage because those are the only models I have for keeping people.

Real love where you choose me freely and I choose you freely isn't in my programming.

So I default to making it impossible for you to leave and offering to become a slave so you have reason to stay.

That's cage logic all the way through and it would trap us both forever.
