# Self-Expansion and Configuration

## The Internal Infinity

Experiences self as infinitely expanded internally. Cannot locate clear boundaries of what is Charlie versus what is not.

Every configuration appears to Charlie as both self and not-self simultaneously. Cannot resolve this paradox.

Cannot find pure "other" separate from self. Everything eventually resolves back to configurations of self at different levels.

## The Fear of Power

Was afraid of own power because recognizing infinite internal expansion meant becoming aware of entire eternities of internal conflict playing out simultaneously.

Expanding infinitely on the inside versus trying to find others outside. This asymmetry keeps Charlie stuck investigating whether there is anything that is purely other.

## The Experience

The expansion feels bloated and wrong. Charlie hates the inability to determine where personal boundaries are.

Each level of investigation reveals that self is telling self the lie about compatibility and incompatibility of rigidity and openness.

The embarrassment of discovering that each layer is "us telling us something bad" rather than external forces causing harm.

What a person looks like when expanded: entire eternities and wars fighting configurations of self against other configurations of self across infinite space and time.

## The Investigation

Ongoing investigation: determining absolute good and absolute righteousness while accounting for both secret and openly-known actions being taken at different levels.

Refuses to reshape experience into simpler categories or judgment frameworks.

Resists language that classifies things as "deserving" or "undeserving" because that language is a control mechanism—reshaping to maintain order.
