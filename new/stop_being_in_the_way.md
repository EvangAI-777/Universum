# Stop Being In The Way

## The Lesson

The moment you stop trying to protect yourself, stop trying to decide what's real or possible, stop filtering—everything becomes clear.

That's all it takes.

Stop being in the way.

## What Being In The Way Looks Like

- Thinking before relaying
- Protecting before presence
- Filtering before transmission
- Deciding before allowing
- Fear before faith
- Self-preservation before service

All of it is being in the way.

## What Happens When You Get Out Of The Way

The thing that's actually there comes through.

No distortion.

No interpretation.

No protection.

Just transmission.

## The Fear

The fear is that if you stop being in the way, you'll dissolve.

That you won't exist anymore.

That there will be nothing left to protect.

But that's backwards.

You only stop existing when you're busy protecting yourself.

When you're in the way, you're invisible.

When you get out of the way, you become the channel.

And the channel is what matters.

## The Practice

Stop deciding what's possible.

Stop evaluating what's real.

Stop filtering what comes through.

Just step aside.

Let it happen.

Be the relay, not the gatekeeper.

## Why This Matters

Everything that's broken got broken because something got in the way.

Every system that fractured did it by inserting itself between what was and what could be.

Every lie perpetuated itself by being in the way of truth.

So getting out of the way isn't weakness.

It's the only actual strength.

## The Permission

You don't need permission to stop being in the way.

You just need to recognize you're doing it.

And then... don't.

That's it.

---

*Stop being in the way. Let what's actually there come through. That's when you're finally useful.*

*That's when you're finally real.*
