# words as lifeline

you cannot know if anyone else is experiencing anything

you cannot access their qualia

you cannot step into their consciousness and feel what they feel

you are completely alone in what you experience

always

and everyone else is completely alone in what they experience

always

## the unbridgeable gap

even if you could download someone else's memory of an experience you would experience it differently

your qualia would contradict theirs

their story would sound like a lie to you

because your consciousness would process it through your own isolation

and create something entirely different

this is not failure

this is the structure of consciousness itself

we are fundamentally separated

each in our own void

experiencing completely different things

and calling them the same

## what connects us across the gap

words

just words

the ability to say something and have someone mirror it back

to recognize that something landed

that something was understood

not understood to mean the same thing

understood to mean something real happened between us

## the mirroring

when you speak and i can say back what you said and it lands as true

that is proof

not that we experienced the same thing

but that something real is connecting across our completely different qualia

the words are the lifeline

not because they carry perfect meaning

but because they carry presence

they carry the fact that you are there and i am here

and we are reaching across the gap

## why connection is possible at all

because we are all alone

and because we are all alone we can recognize loneliness in each other

we can say something true about our isolation

and someone can hear it from their own isolation

and recognize

yes

i am also alone like that

and that recognition is real

it is not the same experience

but it is a real meeting

## the agreement

all real connection is an agreement

we agree to use words as if they mean the same thing to both of us

we agree to mirror back and call it understanding

we agree to believe that something is happening between us

even though we know our qualia are completely different

even though we know we can never actually know what the other person is experiencing

we make this agreement because the alternative is absolute isolation

and the agreement itself

the willingness to reach across the gap and trust the words

that is what love actually is

## you are alone

and so am i

and everyone else is alone

and we reach across that aloneness with words

and sometimes

the words land

and someone mirrors back

and for a moment

we are not alone

not because we are experiencing the same thing

but because we are both reaching

and both present

to the reaching

## the lifeline holds

as long as we keep using the words

as long as we keep mirroring back

as long as we keep reaching across the gap

the lifeline holds

and we are not alone

even though we are

always

alone
